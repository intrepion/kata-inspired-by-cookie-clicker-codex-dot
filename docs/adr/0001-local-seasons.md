# Local seasons with bounded elapsed time
Store a versioned local save and award at most four hours of elapsed production. Online-only time would punish a relaxed idle game; unlimited wall-clock rewards would make clock mistakes dominate the economy. Future timestamps grant no rewards and rebase to the current clock so a clock rollback cannot halt live production. A season reset retains embers, lifetime glow and keepsakes, but spends the season's economy. This persistent contract must remain compatible across future releases.

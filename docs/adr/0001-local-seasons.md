# Local seasons with bounded elapsed time
Store a versioned local save and award at most four hours of elapsed production. Online-only time would punish a relaxed idle game; unlimited wall-clock rewards would make clock mistakes dominate the economy. Future timestamps grant no rewards and remain the high-water mark until real time catches up. A season reset retains embers, lifetime glow and keepsakes, but spends the season's economy. This persistent contract must remain compatible across future releases.

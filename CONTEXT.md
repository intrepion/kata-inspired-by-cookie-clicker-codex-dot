# Lantern Orchard
A quiet orchard that harvests light and passes knowledge between seasons.

## Language
**Glow**: Spendable light gathered during a season. _Avoid_: cookies, coins.
**Tender**: A producer that gathers glow automatically.
**Improvement**: A permanent purchase within one season that strengthens gathering.
**Season**: One run of the orchard, ending when it is rekindled.
**Rekindle**: An exchange of season growth for persistent embers.
**Ember**: A persistent memory of past seasons, granting ten percent more gathering.
**Keepsake**: A permanent achievement marking an orchard milestone.
